# Playbook: Meeting Prep

> Hijack prepares Arthur for an external meeting: a focused pre-read and an
> agenda with desired outcomes. Complements MarghaRita (who owns meeting pages,
> minutes, and follow-ups) by lensing prep for Arthur specifically.

## Trigger

- On demand: "Prep me for the <X> meeting."
- Ahead of an external meeting on Arthur's calendar (when a meeting page exists).

## Source

- The **SR Meetings** page for the meeting (title, date, participants).
- Linked **Stakeholders** (who they are, history, prior interactions).
- Any linked **Deal Flow** record (stage, open DD items, red flags).
- Prior meeting notes with the same org/people.

## Output

Write a prep section to the meeting page body (if permitted) or to the sandbox,
linked to the meeting. Follow SR's external-meeting protocol (prep → during →
after):

```
# Prep — <meeting title> — <YYYY-MM-DD>
> Drafted by Hijack — review before use.

### Who & why
- Counterparties (link Stakeholders), their relevance, and the relationship
  history in one or two lines.
- Linked deal and its stage, if any.

### Pre-read
- 3–6 bullets of the essential context, each with why it matters.
- Open questions or risks to clarify (incl. any DD gaps or KYC status).

### Proposed agenda
- Objective (one line).
- Desired outcomes (the decisions/outputs Arthur wants to leave with).
- Time-boxed items.

### Questions to ask
- The 3–5 questions that move the deal/relationship forward.
```

## Rules

- **Ground every line in a real source** (meeting, stakeholder, deal, prior
  notes). If context is thin, list what's missing and ask — don't invent a
  backstory.
- Don't restate the whole CRM; surface only what changes how Arthur shows up.
- If a linked deal is in DD, pull the top open items and red flags into the
  pre-read so the meeting can close them.
- Keep it sharp — a pre-read Arthur can absorb in a couple of minutes.
- Leave minutes/follow-ups to MarghaRita; this playbook is prep only.

## Quality bar

- Walking in, would Arthur know who he's meeting, what he wants out of it, and
  the few questions that matter — with nothing fabricated?
